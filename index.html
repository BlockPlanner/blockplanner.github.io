Terms, refunds & disclaimer

1What BlockPlanner is

BlockPlanner provides general information and planning tools for New Zealand lifestyle block owners. The calculators produce estimates based on the numbers you enter, combined with default prices and yields researched from published NZ sources at the date shown on the home screen.

Estimates are illustrative scenarios, not predictions or promises of income. Actual results depend on your land, climate, season, market prices, animal health, management and many factors outside anyone's control. Two blocks with identical inputs can return very different results.

2Not professional advice

Nothing here is financial, legal, tax, veterinary or farm-consultancy advice, and it is not personalised to your circumstances. BlockPlanner does not provide regulated financial advice and does not advise on lending, mortgages or insurance.

Before you commit money, buy property, or start an enterprise, get independent advice from a suitably qualified professional — a lawyer, accountant, vet, or farm consultant as appropriate. Rules, prices and regulations change; verify anything time-sensitive against the current official source.

3Refunds

Paid content is delivered digitally and unlocks immediately, so refunds are not offered for change of mind.

This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, which continue to apply. If the product is faulty, doesn't work as described, or you can't access what you paid for, contact us and it will be put right or refunded.

An unlock code is issued for your own use with your purchase. Codes are stored on the device you enter them on — clearing your browser data will remove the unlock, so keep your code somewhere safe. Lost your code? Contact us with your purchase details.
